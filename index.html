01 GET TO KNOW SYRIA

A country where
cultures come together.

Syria is located in Western Asia, near the Mediterranean Sea. Its location has connected different civilizations for thousands of years. Across the country, you can find historic cities, traditional markets, old stone buildings, and communities with their own customs and traditions.

This student website introduces Syria through its history, geography, food, and everyday life. It also explores the different communities and traditions that make the country unique.
