01 PLACES TO EXPLORE
01 / CITY

Damascus

Damascus is known for its old streets, traditional houses, busy markets, and skilled craftspeople. Its historic centre offers a glimpse into the city's long and fascinating past.

History and architecture
02 / ANCIENT SITE

Palmyra

Located in the Syrian desert, Palmyra was once an important oasis and trading centre. Its ancient ruins show the influence of different civilizations that passed through the region.

Ancient history
03 / COASTAL CITY

Latakia

Latakia sits along the Mediterranean coast. The city is associated with its harbour, sea views, nearby green hills, and food influenced by the coastal region.

Coast and nature
04 / NORTHERN CITY

Aleppo

Aleppo has a long history as a centre for trade and craftsmanship. Its historic citadel, traditional markets, and well-known cuisine are important parts of Syrian heritage.

Markets and food
02 DISCOVER SYRIAN FOOD

Made for sharing.
Full of flavour.

Food is an important part of everyday life in Syria. Recipes often pass from one generation to the next, with each family adding its own touch. Depending on the region, meals may include fresh herbs, vegetables, grains, olive oil, and aromatic spices.

01

Kibbeh

Kibbeh is a popular dish made in different ways, often using bulgur wheat and finely ground meat. It can be shaped, stuffed, baked, or fried, depending on the recipe.

02

Fattoush

Fattoush is a fresh salad made with vegetables, herbs, and pieces of toasted or fried flatbread. Sumac adds a distinctive tangy flavour.

03

Maqluba

Maqluba is a layered rice dish cooked with vegetables and sometimes meat. It is traditionally turned upside down onto a serving plate before being brought to the table.

04

Baklava

Baklava is a sweet pastry made with thin layers of dough, nuts, and syrup. It is enjoyed in Syria and many other countries across the region.
